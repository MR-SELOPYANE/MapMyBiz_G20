📘 Module 13: Business Communication & Negotiation

Learn how to speak confidently, clearly, and professionally in every business situation

🎯 Learning Objectives

In this module, you will learn how to communicate clearly and effectively with customers, suppliers, and partners. You’ll discover how to write professional messages using tools like WhatsApp, SMS, or email, so your business is taken seriously. You’ll also learn practical techniques for negotiating better prices, payment terms, or delivery conditions. We’ll show you how to handle difficult conversations with confidence and respect, whether it’s about late payments or product quality. Most importantly, you’ll build trust through honest, consistent, and respectful communication — a key skill for growing any business.

💬 1. Clear Communication Builds Trust

How you speak and write has a big impact on how seriously people take your business. Being clear, polite, and prompt helps you build credibility and strong relationships.

Always say what you mean, so there’s no confusion. Use simple language and be specific about times, amounts, and expectations. Politeness goes a long way — even a simple “please” and “thank you” can make someone more willing to work with you. Try to reply to messages within 24 hours when possible. This shows you are reliable and value the other person’s time.

For example, instead of saying “I’ll bring it when I can,” which is vague, say “I’ll deliver your order tomorrow between 10 AM and 12 PM.” This version is clear, professional, and builds trust.

📱 2. Professional Messaging (WhatsApp, SMS, Email)

You don’t need to sound formal to be professional. Just be clear, respectful, and organized in your messages.

Start with a friendly greeting that includes your name and business, like “Hi Mr. Jacobs, this is Thandi from Thandi’s Bakery.” Then get straight to the point — for example, “I’m following up on the flour order we discussed.” End with a clear next step, such as “Please let me know if this works for you.”

Hi Nomsa,
This is Jabu from Soweto Repairs. Just confirming your phone is ready for pickup today between 3–5 PM.
Total: R350 cash on collection.
Thanks for trusting us with your repair!

This kind of message is polite, gives all the details, and leaves a good impression.

🤝 3. How to Negotiate Like a Pro

Negotiation is not about winning — it’s about finding a solution where both sides benefit. You can negotiate with suppliers on prices, payment terms, or delivery times to make your business more affordable and flexible.

For example, you might ask for a discount if you buy in bulk, suggest paying half now and half later, or request an earlier delivery to meet customer demand. The key is to approach the conversation with respect and preparation.

Before negotiating, know the fair market price so you don’t ask for something unreasonable. Be polite and show interest in their product — for instance, say “I really like your product. Is there any flexibility on price?” You can also offer value in return, such as paying cash today or placing a larger order, in exchange for a better deal.

🛒 Real-Life Example: Negotiating Stock Prices

You: “Hi, I’m starting a small snack business. I’d like to buy 100 packets of chips. The usual price is R8, but I see others get R7 in bulk. Can I get R7 if I pay cash today?”

Supplier: “Hmm… if you take 120 packets, yes.”

You: “Deal. I’ll come now with cash.”

✅ You saved R120, and the supplier made a bigger sale. This is a win-win outcome made possible through polite, confident communication.

🗣️ 4. Handling Difficult Conversations

Sometimes you need to talk about uncomfortable topics — like a late delivery, poor quality, or a customer complaint. In these moments, staying calm and professional is key.

Avoid blaming the other person. Instead, use “I” statements to express your experience — for example, “I noticed the delivery was late” rather than “You were late.” Focus on finding a solution by asking, “How can we fix this?” And always listen first — let the other person explain their side before responding.

💡 Practice in your head: “I am calm. I am professional. I deserve respect.” This simple mindset shift can help you stay confident during tough talks.

📞 5. Phone & In-Person Communication Tips

When speaking on the phone or in person, speak clearly and take your time. Don’t rush your words — pause between sentences so the other person can follow. Smiling while you talk can actually change your tone and make you sound friendlier, even over the phone.

Prepare what you want to say before making a call. Knowing your key points helps you stay focused and confident. During the conversation, take notes — especially on agreements like delivery times or payment amounts.

After the call, send a quick message to confirm what was agreed. For example: “Just confirming — we agreed on R900, paid 50% upfront.” This prevents misunderstandings and shows professionalism.

🌟 6. Build Confidence Over Time

You don’t have to be perfect from the start — confidence grows with practice. Begin with small steps, like sending one professional message per week or asking one polite negotiation question.

After each conversation, reflect on what went well and what you could improve. You can even ask for feedback — for example, “Was my quote clear? Is there anything else you need?” This shows you care and are committed to doing better.

Remember: every great business owner started exactly where you are — learning as they went. With every message, call, and negotiation, you’re building the skills that will help your business grow.
